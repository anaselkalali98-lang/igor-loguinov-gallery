03 — About the artist

IL

Between the
real and the
remembered.

Igor Loguinov is a contemporary artist whose practice is rooted in painting, drawing, and the quiet poetry of observation. Born in the former Soviet Union and now working between cultures, his work finds a shared vocabulary in the places we carry with us.

Figures, animals, buildings, and symbols appear as actors in a private theatre — not to explain the world, but to make its strangeness visible.
